Privacy Policy

App: Clip Uploader · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Clip Uploader is a personal tool run by one person on their own computer. It uploads short videos made by its owner to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up or use it.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok gives the app:

  • an access token and refresh token, so it can upload videos to that account;
  • basic profile information (the account's display name), to confirm which account is connected.

The app only uses the permissions needed to upload videos as drafts or posts to the connected account (user.info.basic, video.upload and, if enabled later, video.publish).

How the data is used

The tokens are only used to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account and to show which account is connected. The app does not read, collect or analyse other users' data, followers, messages or viewing history.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file that is not uploaded anywhere or shared. No data is stored on any server run by this app.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok (and the other platforms the owner connects), as part of uploading videos.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or by deleting the local token file. After that, the app can no longer access the account.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than its adult owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
